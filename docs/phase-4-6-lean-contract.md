# Phases 4–6 Lean Implementation Contract

## Authority

The approved `<proposed_plan>` in the root Codex conversation dated
18 July 2026 is the complete product and delivery authority for this feedback
initiative. This file is intentionally only a skeleton pointer because the
user required a lean two-role team.

## Permitted team

- `technical-business-analyst`: attempted the additive three-phase planning
  handoff; no other planning or refinement role may be substituted.
- `implement`: the same sole agent sequentially owns implementation, component
  specifications, tests, fixes, self-review, validation reports, documentation,
  commits, and release evidence for Phases 4–6.
- Forbidden: project-manager, competitor-analysis, solutions-architect,
  tech-lead, technical-research, test, debug, review, phase-docs, and all other
  task-agent roles.

## Additive phases

1. **Phase 4 — Trustworthy Planning and Sales:** accessible stock/price
   steppers and end-to-end amended-price revenue correctness.
2. **Phase 5 — Stock Lifecycle and Capacity Intelligence:** schema-v3 batched
   LIFO inventory, three-day expiry, refrigeration extensions, migration,
   weighted planning estimates, live rush stock grid, and expiry reporting.
3. **Phase 6 — Living Rush and Unique People:** deterministic rush activity,
   clearer queue/sale/exit/walkaway animation with reduced-motion parity, and
   campaign-wide non-repeating staff names.

## Locked defaults

- Servable stock is displayed as a deterministic weighted `~N serves` estimate.
- Rolled stock is consumed newest-first (true LIFO).
- Stock is usable on purchase day and two following trading days, expiring
  after the third rush; refrigeration extends configured chilled stock by one
  or two days at tiers 1 and 2.
- Legacy flat inventory migrates as current-day batches with full shelf life.
- Sale feedback shows the actual charge including size and milk surcharges.
- Staff names never repeat after being displayed within the same campaign.
- Every phase runs the exact validation sequence in `docs/project-profile.md`
  and requires desktop plus 360px touch-mobile proof.
